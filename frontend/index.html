Rule-Based EntityRuler vs Statistical NER

Off-the-shelf statistical NER models (e.g. standard en_core_web_sm) are trained on general news corpora. Consequently, they routinely misclassify technical entities (labeling "Docker" as an Organization or missing compound technical phrases like "RAG", "TF-IDF", or "CI/CD").
